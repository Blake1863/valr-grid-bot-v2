### What happened (early AM)

  - CMS2: USDC $76.01 → $14.00, USDT $24.43 → $90.14

Swap endpoint: `POST /v1/orders/market` with `{pair:"USDTUSDC", side:"BUY", quoteAmount:"55"}` — treats USDC as quote to buy USDT base.

### ROOT CAUSE (important — revisit before next fix)
**It's not a balance problem. It's a strategy design problem.**

The bot is a **wash-trader** (per README) — CMS1 and CMS2 post opposite BUY/SELL to self-match, creating chart volume. In theory net inventory/USDT flat. In practice both accounts are **net-accumulating base AND net-losing USDT**, i.e. the other side of trades is going to **external market participants**, not the partner account.

Traced MSTRXUSDT over 6h:
- CMS1: +0.006 MSTRX
- CMS2: +0.058 MSTRX
Both up = both sides BUY-heavy. Makers getting eaten externally before partner taker arrives.

### Why illiquid pairs fail
Between maker placement and taker placement, either:
1. External trader hits the maker first → inventory lost to real market
2. Best bid/ask moves → taker gets cancelled (insufficient balance / post-only violation)

Self-match only works when spread stays stable for ~100ms+ — fails on wide-spread illiquid pairs (TSLAX, VALR10, CRCLX, BITGOLD, HOODX, MSTRX, JUP, PUMP).

### ZAR pairs are dead
All ZAR pairs (BTCZAR, ETHZAR, SOLZAR, XRPZAR, AVAXZAR, BNBZAR, LINKZAR) enabled in config.json but both accounts have only ~R6 ZAR. 100+ Insufficient Balance errors. Just burning cycles — should be disabled or the accounts need ZAR funded.

### Rebalancer limitation
`spot_rebalance_manual.py` only rebalances >60% splits BETWEEN accounts. Cannot detect **total pool shrinkage to external market**. MSTRX/TSLAX/JUP/VALR10 rarely trigger rebalances (split stays near 50/50 because both accounts lose symmetrically to the outside).

## Lessons
- **Wash/self-match strategies are fragile on illiquid pairs.** External market activity breaks the self-match; "maker cancel rate" is the canary.
- **"Rebalance between accounts" ≠ "refill inventory"**. If both accounts drain together, rebalancer sees nothing wrong.
- **USDTUSDC swap works as USDC→USDT bridge** — `POST /v1/orders/market` with `quoteAmount`. Useful when replenisher's PUMP route is broken.
- **Always check SELL cancel rate per pair before trusting a wash bot is "working."** Fills alone lie; cancel-rate-on-the-generating-side tells the truth.

## UPDATE 04:30 SGT — Fix DEPLOYED (Option C variant)

Built **`cm-bot-spot-illiquid`** — Python self-cross orchestrator. Shipped and live.

### What it does
Per cycle (every 20s, staggered 2s between pairs):
1. Fetch `/v1/public/{pair}/orderbook` → best_bid, best_ask
2. Pick maker (CMS1 or CMS2, alternating per cycle) and direction (SELL then BUY alternating)
3. Place maker `postOnly: true` limit **inside** the spread (one tick inside from the chosen side)
4. Immediately fire partner taker as IOC limit at the same price
5. Self-match → chart prints. If external trader beat us: maker fills externally, taker IOC cancels cleanly.

### Files
- `bots/cm-bot-spot-illiquid/bot.py` — single-file bot, stdlib only
- `bots/cm-bot-spot-illiquid/config.json` — 8 pairs enabled, cycle=20s

### Bugs fixed during deploy
1. **Inventory floor check inverted logic**: compared `maker_bal * price < max(notional, floor)`, so a $2.60 inventory with floor=$3 blocked every trade even though the trade only needed $1. Fixed: separate hard-check (`bal >= qty * 1.02`) from soft warning (`bal_value < floor_warn`). BITGOLD was silently skipping all cycles because of this.

### Design notes for future-me
- **Maker inside spread, not improving a side that faces external pressure** — I considered `bestAsk - tick` vs `bestBid + tick` risk differences. Conclusion: external-trader exposure is symmetric in that narrow window (~50ms). What matters is: (a) maker is post-only so can't accidentally cross, (b) taker IOC so can't rest, (c) both fire back-to-back with no WS wait.
- **Minimum 2 ticks of spread required** to safely place inside without crossing. Pairs checked had 13-93 ticks.
- Per-pair `PairState` tracks prints/fails/skips → logged every 5 min as summary.
- `_split_pair(pair)` assumes quote currency is one of USDTPERP/USDCPERP/ZARPERP/USDT/USDC/ZAR/BTC/ETH. Add more if ever needed.
